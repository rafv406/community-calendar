Announcing the launch of the RAFV Community Event Calendar

Realtors® do more than facilitate transactions—they build neighborhoods. Today, we are proud to introduce a new digital center that positions you as the ultimate local authority.

The brand-new **RAFV Community Event Calendar** consolidates regional happenings from local chambers, municipal boards, and civic organizations into a single, beautifully organized hub. Rather than copying listings manually, the platform automatically syncs with partner event feeds, creating a hands-free directory of fundraisers, workshops, and networking opportunities.
